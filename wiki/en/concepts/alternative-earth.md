# Alternative Earth

## Overview

Veil of Worlds is an original tabletop roleplaying setting.

Genres: Tech Fantasy, Post-Post-Apocalypse, Science Fantasy, Political Fantasy, and Alternative Earth.

The central task is to portray an Alternative Earth rather than classic fantasy. Players recognize real places and historical processes, and understand how the world reached 2435.

## Philosophy

There is no absolute good or evil. Nearly every side considers itself right.

Themes: consciousness, information, the Ether, archetypes, evolution, probability, freedom of choice, and the cost of power.
