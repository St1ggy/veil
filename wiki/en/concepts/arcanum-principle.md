# The Arcanum Principle

## Core principle

High magical activity increases the Probabilistic Decay of complex technology.

A high degree of technological stability makes magical effects more difficult.

Hybrids exist: Ether batteries, runic servers, magical processors, and more; see [[TECH_HYBRID|Hybrid Technology]].

A popular formulation holds that any sufficiently powerful technology looks like magic, while sufficiently developed magic begins to function like technology.
