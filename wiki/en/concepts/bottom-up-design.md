# Bottom-Up Design Principle

## Overview

The world is built from the bottom up. Elements cannot be created merely for variety.

Every new entity must:

- follow logically from canon;
- solve a world problem or create a conflict;
- open play opportunities;
- affect at least two systems (politics and economics, magic and society, and so on).

If an entity can be removed without noticeable consequences, it should not exist.

Every element must affect history, economics, politics, religion, culture, society, technology, and/or magic.
