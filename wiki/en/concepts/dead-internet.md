# Dead Internet

## Overview

The global Internet has been destroyed (see [[EVENT_THIRD_WORLD_WAR|Third World War]]).

The Ether preserved informational traces of the former Network. This layer is called the Dead Internet.

It supports the practice of [[MAG_NETWORK|Network Mages]] and contact with AI remnants.

## Open questions

- What is the structure of the Dead Internet’s “layers”?
- Can a stable global network be restored without the Ether?
