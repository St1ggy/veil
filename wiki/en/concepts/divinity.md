# Divinity

## Overview

Gods are not a separate species. Divinity is a state of being.

Any sapient being can potentially become a god: human, elf, orc, dwarf, construct, AI, or any other thinking entity.

There is no single path to Ascension. Every case is unique.

## Loss

Divinity is not final. It can be lost through dethronement, voluntary renunciation, loss of a Domain, defeat by another god, or the conditions of Loss.

Afterward, a former god may remain immortal, become mortal, lose their memory, become a spirit, or become an ordinary human. Each case is individual.
