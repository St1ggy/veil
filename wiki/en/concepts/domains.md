# Domains

## Overview

A god is bound to one or more Domains, the world’s fundamental concepts.

Examples include Memory, War, Probability, Sea, Machines, Dream, Time, Order, Path, and Knowledge.

## Politics of Domains

A Domain can be gained, lost, inherited, taken, divided, or combined.

This makes gods part of world politics rather than an external pantheon above it.
