# Archetypes

## Definition

Archetypes are stable forms of the collective unconscious that exist in the Ether.

As the Veil weakens, they begin to materialize: myths acquire bodies, peoples, and entities.

The link to gods and new peoples is indirect: an archetype can become a path to a Domain or a people’s source of origin, but does not do so automatically.
