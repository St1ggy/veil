# Ether

## Definition

The Ether is a fundamental layer of reality beyond ordinary perception. It is tied to probabilities, ideas, archetypes, and collective consciousness.

The Ether is not “energy” in the everyday sense. It does not directly create the impossible.

It changes the probability that the impossible becomes possible.

## Chain of influence

1. Consciousness affects the Ether.
2. The Ether affects probability.
3. Probability affects the Material World.

This is the foundation of all magic in Veil.
