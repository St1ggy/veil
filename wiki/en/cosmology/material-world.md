# Material World

## Definition

The Material World is the Earth and the campaign’s physical reality.

The map is recognizable: the Alps, Amazonia, Prague, Tokyo, New York, the Carpathians, Scandinavia, the U.S. Midwest, and other familiar landmarks remain geographical anchors.

Borders, climate, biomes, and states have changed, but human history still feels continuous.
