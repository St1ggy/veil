# The Veil

## Definition

The Veil separates the Material World from the Ether.

Before 2170, it almost completely blocked interaction.

In 2170, the weakening began. Breaches appeared. By 2435, the Veil is weakened but not destroyed.

## Politics

Restoring the Veil is the goal of the [[ORG_PURE_CODE|Order of Pure Code]].

Final merger with the Ether is the goal of the [[ORG_RETURN_CULT|Cult of Return]].
