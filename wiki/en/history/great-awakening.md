# Great Awakening

## Overview

In 2170, the Veil began to weaken. The Ether entered the Material World. Archetypes began to materialize. The first mages and unexplained phenomena appeared.

## Date and scope

- Date: 2170
- Scope: global

## Consequences (the following decades)

- new peoples emerge;
- ancient entities awaken;
- Ether zones appear;
- ecosystems change;
- humanity attempts to combine technology and magic.
