# Starfall

## Overview

Many objects remain in orbit. Sometimes they fall.

The falls contain AIs, new materials, artifacts, and technology. They become targets for scavengers.

The phenomenon is tied to the destruction of orbital infrastructure during the [[EVENT_THIRD_WORLD_WAR|Third World War]] and continues in the era of 2435.
