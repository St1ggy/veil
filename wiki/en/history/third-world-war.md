# Third World War

## Overview

The year 2325. The Third World War.

## Consequences

- destruction of the global economy;
- collapse of most states;
- destruction of orbital infrastructure;
- [[EVENT_STARFALL|Starfall]];
- destruction of the global Internet, creating the conditions for the [[CONCEPT_DEAD_INTERNET|Dead Internet]].

By 2435, the world had recovered—but it was now a different civilization.
