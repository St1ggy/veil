# Ether Burnout

## Overview

The source of magical work is the mage’s neural activity.

Using magic causes:

- fatigue;
- emotional exhaustion;
- personality change;
- memory loss;
- mutations;
- Ether Burnout as its extreme form.

The more powerful the mage, the higher the cost.

## Social consequences

It limits the mass use of magic, creates demand for hybrid technology and anti-magic measures, and affects medicine and law.

## Open questions

- Is complete Burnout reversible?
- Are there institutions that legally “write off” mages?
