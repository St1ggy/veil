# Ocean Ether Storms

## Overview

Most oceans are dangerous. Ether storms occur there.

Safe sea corridors exist, but they shift constantly.

This makes maritime trade an expertise of [[COUNTRY_BALTIC_HARBORS|the Baltic Harbors Confederation]] and a source of political influence.
