# Probabilistic Decay

## Overview

Magic does not disable electronics. It increases the probability of faults.

## Consequences

- processors make errors;
- SSDs lose data;
- lasers are unstable;
- weapons misfire;
- batteries discharge faster;
- engines perform worse.

This effect is called Probabilistic Decay.

## Relationship to the Arcanum principle

[[CONCEPT_ARCANUM|The Arcanum principle]] describes the mutual pressure of magic and advanced technology; Probabilistic Decay is its observed mechanism on the technological side.
