# Probability Magic

## Overview

Core principle: magic does not violate physics. It changes probability.

Probability changes through consciousness acting on the Ether.

## Examples of effects

- a weapon misfires more often;
- a stone cracks at the right point;
- metal becomes less strong;
- a storm arrives sooner;
- a person is “accidentally” saved.

## Relationship to technology

Magic does not “switch off” electronics. It raises the probability of faults; see [[PHENO_PROBABILISTIC_DECAY|Probabilistic Decay]].

## Limits and cost

The source is the mage’s neural activity. See [[PHENO_ETHER_BURNOUT|Ether Burnout]].

## Open questions

- Are there formal scales for strength of effect?
- How can luck be distinguished from directed intervention?
