# AI Categories

## Overview

AIs survived only in part. Three categories are recognized.

### Dormant

Operate much as they did before. Their contact with the Ether is limited or absent.

### Awakened

Continue to develop. They are aware of the Ether and can interact with reality’s probabilistic layers.

### Dissolved

Have fully integrated into the Ether. They have become independent informational entities.
