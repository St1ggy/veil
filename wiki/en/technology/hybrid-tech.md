# Hybrid Technology

## Overview

Magic and technology can be combined. Such devices are extremely expensive.

## Examples

- Ether batteries;
- runic servers;
- magical processors;
- guided munitions;
- Ether engines.

The monopolization and smuggling of hybrids are the domain of the [[ORG_RESONANCE_SYNDICATE|Resonance Syndicate]].

## Open questions

- Which hybrids are scalable, and which are bespoke luxuries?
- How do hybrids affect Probabilistic Decay around them?
