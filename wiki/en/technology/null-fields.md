# Null Fields

## Overview

Null Fields are a technological response to Probabilistic Decay: they locally suppress or stabilize magical fluctuations.

They are used extensively by [[COUNTRY_AEGIS|Aegis]]. They are ideologically close to the aims of [[ORG_PURE_CODE|the Order of Pure Code]], but are not identical to the Order.
