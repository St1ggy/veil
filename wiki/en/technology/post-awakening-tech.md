# Post-Awakening Technology

## Overview

Technology did not disappear. Electricity, AI, robots, railways, factories, data centers, firearms, drones, and aviation still exist.

They are exceptionally difficult to maintain because of Probabilistic Decay, ruined infrastructure, and the shortage of expertise after 2325.
